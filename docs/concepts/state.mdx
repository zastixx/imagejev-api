# State & Vector Space

ImageJev is entirely stateless and deterministic.

## Single Image Embedding Reuse

In multi-question pipelines, an image is embedded exactly once. If you ask 10 independent questions about the same image:
1. The image is passed through the vision backbone to produce a normalized embedding tensor $v_{img} \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$.
2. All 10 text schemas are embedded into direction matrices.
3. Batch dot products ($v_{img} \cdot W_{text}^T$) evaluate all 10 determinations in parallel in under $0.5\text{ms}$.

## Zero Hallucination Guarantee

Because there is no autoregressive decoder:
- No tokens are generated sequentially.
- The model cannot invent options outside of your schema.
- The decision is mathematically bounded within the simplex formed by the provided options.
